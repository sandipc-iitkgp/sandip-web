Who can register

  • Any IIT KGP alumnus, from any batch and any department, currently working in CSE, IT or a related computing field.
  • Especially relevant if you are looking to give back, mentor students, support research, or engage with the startup ecosystem.
  • One guest each, to be confirmed. A spouse or partner is welcome.

Why come

  • Reconnect with campus peers now driving tech in Bengaluru.
  • Support department research and undergraduate projects.
  • Take part in professional upskilling and hackathons.

  • Spend an evening with people who share the same campus memories.
